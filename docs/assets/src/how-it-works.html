How it works

Your YT Music likes are a view of your YouTube likes

YouTube Liked videos is where every like is stored. YT Music Liked songs shows the same list, in the same order, but each entry may play a different video than the like behind it. like-surgeon lines the two lists up by position to find out which like is behind each song.
